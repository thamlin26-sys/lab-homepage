Physical Requirements to Become an Army Ranger

To become an Army Ranger, you must meet the following physical requirements:

PT Requirements

Standing 5m Run Push-ups Pull-ups
Great under 35:00 57+ 16+
AVG 35:00 - 36:00 51 - 56 13 - 15
Just Passing 36:00 - 40:00 41 - 50 6 - 12

These benchmarks are used to evaluate your physical readiness for the demands of Army Ranger training. In passing these standards, the US Army ensures that only the most qualified candidates advance to the rigorous training program designed for the most elite soldiers under combat.

Army Rangers are known for their physical fitness, mental toughness, and ability to work as a team. Click the next page to see the pipeline of becoming an Army Ranger!
